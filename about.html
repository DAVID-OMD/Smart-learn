About SmartLearn

SmartLearn is a lightweight learning website for secondary school students. It provides short notes and practice questions for core subjects without requiring logins, databases, or internet access.

Our Goals

  • Make revision quick and accessible
  • Keep content clear and curriculum-friendly
  • Work on any device with a web browser

How It Works

  1. Go to the Subjects page.
  2. Choose a subject like Math or Science.
  3. Read the summary and click “Show Practice Set”.

For Teachers & Parents

All files are plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. You can copy the folder to any computer or USB drive and open index.html to run it.
